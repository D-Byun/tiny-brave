Tiny Brave privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Tiny Brave is a small-steps app that helps people practice being brave. This page explains what the app keeps, what leaves your phone, and the choices you and your grown-ups have.

The solo app

Your progress, balloon, drawings and recordings stay on your device. We never receive them. The microphone is only used when you tap start recording, and recordings are not uploaded.

Circles (optional)

Circles let up to six friends cheer each other on. They are only available to people 13 and older. If you use them, we store:

  • a random anonymous ID created by the app (there is no email, password or real name);
  • a nickname you choose, your brave count and your streak;
  • your friends list, friend requests, blocks and reactions;
  • an age band (under 13, 13–15 or 16+). Your exact birthday never leaves your phone. The age band comes from Apple's Declared Age Range or from a birth month and year you enter on the device;
  • a notification token, if you allow notifications, so friends' cheers can reach you.

Circles never carry free text, photos, drawings or recordings. Friends only see a short preset message, your nickname and your count.

Posts, feed entries and reactions are deleted automatically after 30 days, and friend requests after 14 days.

Children under 13

People under 13 can use the solo app. Circles stay off for them, and we do not collect personal information from them for circles. If we offer circles to under-13s in the future, we will first get verifiable consent from a parent or guardian.

Notifications

Notifications are optional, limited to 10 a day, can be muted per friend, and are quiet from 9 pm to 7 am.

Who we share data with

Circle data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and App Check) on our behalf. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Your choices

In the app you can choose "Delete my circle data". This removes your friends, requests, posts, reactions, codes and anonymous account from our servers. You can also delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. A parent or guardian can contact us to ask for deletion at any time.
